Animated preview

Windows now open the default way: side by side at equal widths. Every step after that is a real key press through the plugin's own bindings, recorded in a disposable Hyprland session. The capture is 2:1 (1920 x 960). The video here is compressed to fit the page.

What the loop shows

  1. Four windows open one at a time. Each new window gets an equal column.
  2. A fifth window opens next to the focused one. Five equal columns.
  3. Super Shift → twice builds a group on the right, then Super Shift ↑ stacks it: P | T | (Y / (X | D)).
  4. Super Shift ↑ twice on the bottom-right window makes an equal stack of three: P | T | (Y / D / X).
  5. Super Alt P on the middle column gives it half the width. Pressing it again restores equal tiles.

Changes from your layouts

  • Your third layout had the right group wider than the two left columns. I could not reach that width with key presses alone, so the loop uses the equal version.
  • Your last layout needs closing two windows first. The loop shows the priority column with all five windows open instead.
